## 📌 Project Overview

AlloyTower Inc. was a collaborative project focused on designing a centralized data platform for a real estate company.

The project addressed challenges associated with fragmented property data, inconsistent information, inefficient workflows, and limited access to market insights.

**My role focused on data analytics and dashboard development**, contributing to the presentation of business information and insights to support data-driven decision-making.

## 🎯 Business Challenges

- Fragmented property and market data across platforms
- Inconsistent information and data quality issues
- Limited access to timely market insights
- Inefficient property and customer inquiry management
- Delays in business decision-making

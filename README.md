# 🏢 Real Estate Analytics & Dashboard Development

### Property Intelligence | Data Visualization | Business Insights

A collaborative business analysis project focused on designing a centralized data platform concept for a real estate company. The project explored how property listings, customer inquiries, and market data could be brought together to improve information access and business reporting.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- **Centralize information:** Bring property listings, customer inquiries, and market data into a proposed centralized platform.
- **Improve information access:** Address fragmented data and manual workflows.
- **Support decision-making:** Use dashboard visualizations to make business information easier to interpret.
- **Improve reporting:** Present relevant information in a clear, structured format for stakeholders.

## 🔍 Project Focus

**📊 Data Analytics**

Contributed to the analysis of business information to support reporting and insights.

**📈 Dashboard Development**

Contributed to dashboard design and visualization to present information in a clear, accessible format.

**🤝 Collaborative Business Analysis**

Worked as part of a team exploring requirements and the proposed centralized platform concept.
